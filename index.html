Executive Summary

Q4 2025 delivered exceptional results across all digital channels, with strong business impact and outstanding return on investment.

Strategic Recommendations for Q1 2026

1
Scale TikTok Strategy

Formalize TikTok advertising with proper tracking. The platform delivered 5,572 followers organically - paid amplification could double this growth. Allocate R3,000-5,000 for Q1.

2
Optimize Mobile Experience

With 51.8% mobile traffic, implement mobile-first design improvements, WhatsApp Business integration, and streamlined checkout to convert high-intent mobile visitors.

3
Product-Focused Content

Scale the successful product showcase format. Create similar videos for top-performing categories: Catering (288 visits), Pots & Pans (198), Glassware (148).

4
Retargeting Campaign

Implement Facebook/Instagram retargeting for the 4,000+ website visitors. Industry benchmarks show 15-20% conversion lift from retargeting high-intent audiences.
