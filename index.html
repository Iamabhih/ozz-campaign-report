Strategic Recommendations for Q1 2026

1
Scale TikTok with Paid Strategy

Formalize TikTok advertising with R3,000-5,000 monthly budget. The platform delivered 5,572 followers organically - paid amplification could double Q1 growth with proper tracking.

2
Optimize Mobile Conversion Funnel

With 69% Google and 51.8% website mobile traffic, implement WhatsApp Business integration, mobile-optimized checkout, and SMS follow-ups to convert high-intent mobile visitors.

3
Leverage Google Business Dominance

Enhance Google Business Profile with weekly posts, respond to all reviews, add more photos, and use Google Posts feature. Maintain dominant position in "cash and carry durban" searches.

4
Product-Focused Content Scale

Replicate successful TikTok product showcases for top categories: Catering equipment, Pots & Pans, Glassware. Create "How to use" and "Benefits" videos based on top searches.

5
Multi-Channel Retargeting

Implement cross-platform retargeting for the 4,000+ website visitors and 1,715 direction requesters. Use Facebook/Instagram custom audiences and Google Customer Match.

6
Track WhatsApp Conversions

Implement WhatsApp Business API for proper conversion tracking. Given the prominent WhatsApp number in content, manual tracking could reveal 15-30% of sales via this channel.
